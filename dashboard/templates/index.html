Welcome

This dashboard controls the Modernise-TradeMaster reinforcement-learning platform.

What is Reinforcement Learning?

An agent learns a policy by interacting with a market environment, receiving rewards (profit/loss), and improving decisions over episodes to maximize long-term return.

Platform modules

  • Agents — DQN, PPO, SAC, SARL, EIIE, DeepScalper and more
  • Environments — Algorithmic Trading, Order Execution, Portfolio Management
  • Data Extensions — pluggable OHLCV loaders under data_extensions/
  • Experiments — train, validate, backtest and visualize
