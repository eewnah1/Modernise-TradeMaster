Reinforcement Learning at a glance

  • Agent — a policy network (DQN, PPO, SAC, EIIE, SARL, DeepScalper) that decides actions.
  • Environment — a market simulator for AT, OE, or PM tasks that produces rewards.
  • Reward — profit, risk-adjusted return, or execution cost signal used to improve the policy.
  • Episode — one full training run over historical data; the agent learns over many episodes.
